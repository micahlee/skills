# Evidence Standard

Use this hierarchy:

1. Current clinician/PT directions specific to Micah.
2. Clinical practice guidelines and consensus statements from recognized professional bodies.
3. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses.
4. High-quality controlled studies.
5. Reputable hospital, university, or professional educational material.
6. Clearly labeled expert opinion when stronger evidence is unavailable.

For technical or therapy research, use primary/authoritative sources. Record source, publication date, population/applicability, purpose, contraindications/stop conditions, uncertainty, and review date. Do not use influencer content or unattributed generated prose as therapeutic authority.

Use live research when:

- creating the initial block;
- performing the four-week formal review;
- answering a novel injury/test/rehab question;
- an approved policy is plausibly outdated or disputed.

Do not browse daily or for ordinary per-workout adjustments. Daily logic applies the approved evidence library and current state.

Useful starting authorities:

- ACSM resistance-training guidance and position stands;
- IOC consensus on load and injury risk;
- peer-reviewed systematic reviews on concurrent training, tapering, periodization, RIR, and failure training;
- National Weather Service lightning, heat/WBGT, and cold guidance.

State when evidence is indirect, population-specific, low confidence, or being used as a practical inference.
