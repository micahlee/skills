# Weekly Mode

Use for `/personal-sync weekly`, or `/personal-sync` with no explicit mode.

## Attended workflow

1. Resolve the local week and the 7-day look-back plus 14-day look-forward.
2. Read shared sources and prior human/agent weekly notes.
3. Reconcile completed, carried, abandoned, and newly urgent work before proposing anything.
4. Run the capacity helper with recent observations and upcoming constraints.
5. Present a compact `What seems true` synthesis:
   - last-week wins, misses, and lessons
   - predicted capacity
   - fixed constraints and preparation
   - active authoritative goals
   - candidate outcomes and two-week concerns
6. Ask only unresolved decisions. Ask one question at a time, follow dependencies, and include the recommended answer.
7. Normally select no more than the capacity recommendation; each outcome needs a `done enough` definition.
8. Preview the human weekly note, agent weekly note, and day-bound future todos.
9. Wait for explicit approval.
10. Write approved surfaces, sidecars/log, and compact events.

Do not ask questions answerable from sources. Do not treat maintenance as an outcome. Challenge overload by naming the swap, shrink, defer, or risk.

## Look-back

Notice completed outcomes, meaningful partial progress, deliberate releases, repeated carryover, calendar reality, and strong reflection patterns. Avoid grading the week.

## Look-forward

The primary week drives choices. The second week exists for preparation, deadlines, travel, and decisions—not for doubling the active plan.
